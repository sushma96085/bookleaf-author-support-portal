# BookLeaf Support Knowledge Base
- Publishing packages: Free and Bestseller Breakthrough; supports India and US self-publishing.
- Printing: Delhi in-house; overflow partners Repro India and Epitome Books.
- Royalties: authors receive 80% of net profit, BookLeaf 20%. Net profit = MRP - printing - platform commission - shipping. Paid quarterly within 45 days of quarter end; minimum payout INR 1,000; bank transfer.
- ISBN: assigned by BookLeaf. Authors using their own imprint need their own ISBN. Incorrect ISBN should be escalated promptly.
- Printing: typically 5–7 business days. Verified printing-quality issues qualify for free reprint; request photographs as evidence when relevant.
- Distribution: Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK, BookLeaf Store. Listing typically takes 7–10 business days. Stock synchronization/resync may take 24–48 hours.
- Production stages: Manuscript Received → Editing → Cover Design → Typesetting → Proofreading → ISBN Assignment → Printing → Distribution Setup → Published & Live.
- Reply style: empathetic, professional, specific, actionable; never claim an action is already complete without verification.
